Step 3 · Sign in once

The platform never sees your password. For the recommended way, it opens a normal browser window, you sign in there — SSO popup, MFA code, whatever your company uses — and only the resulting session is saved so later runs never hit a login screen.

Choose the sign-in method when you register the website on the Systems & reports page.

Save the automation account safely

Choose Open secure window. A separate secure Windows window collects the username and password and saves them directly in Windows Credential Manager under your Windows account. The password never enters this web page, the database, a log, a recording, or the Recording Coach.
